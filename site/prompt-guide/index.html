How to write prompts for Qwen-Image 2.1

The model does what you write, quite literally. Six rules, each with a real result from a vague prompt and from a clear one. Every image on this page was made by Kokon, first try, same seed on both sides.

The rules apply to Qwen-Image 2.1 wherever you run it, for new images and for edits (2.1 does both in one model, the job Qwen-Image-Edit did for earlier versions): in Kokon, ComfyUI or a hosted API.

Creating a new image

01Say what's happening, and where

With a short prompt, the model draws the subject and fills the rest with the most ordinary choice. The action gets lost.

✕ Vague
a monkey playing basketball

A monkey, sitting on a post, with a football. Nobody is playing.

✓ Clear
Photograph of a monkey dribbling a basketball on an outdoor court, full body, sunny afternoon

The action (dribbling), the place (a court) and the framing (full body). Now it plays.

Describe it like a photo you've already seen: who, doing what, where, in what light.

02Never write "no"

The model reads the words, not the grammar. Writing "no laptop" puts "laptop" in its head.

✕ Negative
Photograph of a minimalist wooden desk by a window, no laptop, no computer

There's the laptop.

✓ Positive
Photograph of a minimalist wooden desk by a window, the desk is clean and empty except for a small potted plant and a ceramic cup

Say what is there, and the rest stays empty.

Instead of "no people", write "an empty beach". Instead of "no text", just don't mention text.

03Text goes in quotes, and short

Qwen-Image is great at lettering. Big titles come out right almost every time. Long small lines are where it slips.

✕ Too much small text
…title "NIGHT JAZZ". Below the moon, smaller text "Friday 24 October · Teatro Verdi · 21:00"…

The small line shows up twice, once with a typo ("24 ocber").

✓ Short and placed
…Only two lines of text: the title "NIGHT JAZZ" in big bold letters at the top, and "24 OCTOBER" in small letters at the bottom.

Exact words in quotes, and where each line goes.

Need a lot of small text? Switch to Quality: at native 2K the long line above came out perfect.

04Details turn "a picture" into your picture

A short prompt isn't wrong, it's just generic. Every detail you add is a decision you make instead of the model.

◐ Fine
a girl riding a bicycle
✓ Yours
Photograph of a young woman riding a vintage blue bicycle along a seaside promenade, her hair blowing in the wind, a basket of flowers on the handlebar, late afternoon golden light, shot from the side, full body
◐ Fine
A dog holding a sign that says I ate your homework
✓ A story
Photograph of a guilty-looking golden retriever sitting on a kitchen floor, holding in its mouth a cardboard sign with handwritten black marker text: "I ATE YOUR HOMEWORK". Torn paper scattered around, soft daylight

The model doesn't get tired of reading. Long prompts in plain sentences work, most important things first.

Editing a photo

Drop in a photo and type the change. Kokon keeps everything you don't mention.

05"Better" means nothing. Say what better is.

The model can't read your mind. Ask it to "improve" a photo and it gives the photo back, almost unchanged.

Original
✕ Vague
make it better

Same room.

✓ Clear
Make the room cozy for a winter evening: dusk outside the window, the floor lamp turned on with warm light, a knitted cream blanket on the armchair, a stack of books and a mug of tea on the rug

Turn "better" into things you can see: warmer light, a blanket, tidier, more contrast, a sunset.

06Name the thing you want changed

When the prompt doesn't say what, the model picks the most eye-catching thing in the photo.

Original
✕ Ambiguous
make it blue

You meant the walls. It painted the armchair.

✓ Named
Paint the wall behind the armchair a dusty blue. Keep the yellow armchair and everything else unchanged.

Too many similar things to name? Use Paint area: brush over it and write only the change.

Things that just work

Once the prompt is clear, the model is happy to do a lot at once.

Five changes in one sentence

Remove the red scooter, make it a rainy evening with wet reflective cobblestones, turn on the café lights, add a ginger cat sitting on a chair, and add an old man with an umbrella walking on the right

"Bottom left corner" means bottom left

Add a small white sailboat on the sea in the bottom left corner. Keep everything else unchanged.

Combine two photos

Place the white sneaker from image 2 on the rug in image 1, in front of the yellow armchair, with natural shadows matching the room light. Keep the room from image 1 unchanged.

Change the whole style

Turn it into a Studio Ghibli style anime illustration, soft painted backgrounds, keep the same scene and composition

Counts and positions

Still life photograph on a wooden kitchen table: three red apples on the left, a single green pear on the right, a white ceramic jug in the middle behind them

A whole scene, every detail

…On the left an old chef with a white headband pours broth into a bowl. On the right two customers in winter jackets, one laughing. Paper lanterns, handwritten menu boards, a cat sleeps on a shelf in the background…

Camera angles

Bird's-eye view photograph, straight down, of a breakfast table: a cappuccino with latte art, two croissants, a glass of orange juice, a folded newspaper

Any style you can name

Watercolor illustration of a fox reading a book under a tree in autumn, soft washes of orange and brown, visible paper texture, children's book style

Cheat sheet

New image

type of image of subject doing what, where, light / time, framing, style

Not every part is needed. Add the ones you care about, most important first. Any language works.

When it goes wrong

ProblemTry
Part of the prompt ignoredDescribe that part more, and put it earlier
Something unwanted appearedRemove its name from the prompt, even after "no"
Text is wrongFewer words, in quotes, or switch to Quality
Close, but not quiteRegenerate: a new seed is a new try
Edit changed too muchAdd "keep everything else unchanged", or paint the area
Edit changed nothingName the object and the new look

Skip tag lists like "masterpiece, 8k, best quality". They don't add quality, they just push everything toward the same dramatic stock-photo look. Describe the look you want in words.

Questions

How do I write a good prompt for Qwen-Image 2.1?

Describe the scene, not just the subject: who or what, doing what, where, in what light, and the framing. "A monkey playing basketball" gave a monkey sitting with a football; "a monkey dribbling a basketball on an outdoor court, full body" gave the game. Plain sentences work, most important things first, and long prompts are fine.

Does Qwen-Image 2.1 support negative prompts?

Not in a useful way. The Turbo model runs at CFG 1, where a separate negative prompt has no effect, and writing "no laptop" in the prompt tends to add a laptop. Describe what should be there instead: "a clean, empty desk with only a plant and a cup".

How do I get correct text in Qwen-Image 2.1 images?

Put the exact words in quotes and say where they go, for example the title "NIGHT JAZZ" in big letters at the top. Big titles come out right almost every time; long lines of small text can be garbled at about 1 megapixel, so use fewer words or render at native 2K.

How should I write edit prompts for Qwen-Image 2.1?

Name the exact thing and the change: "Replace the yellow armchair with a green velvet sofa", "Paint the wall behind the armchair dusty blue". Vague requests like "make it better" return almost the same photo, and "make it blue" changes whatever stands out most. Add "keep everything else unchanged" when an edit changes too much.

Can I ask for several edits in one prompt?

Yes. One sentence removed a scooter, turned the scene into a rainy evening, turned on the lights, added a cat and added a man with an umbrella in a single edit. If one change does not happen, edit the result again.

Can I write Qwen-Image prompts in other languages?

Yes. A descriptive prompt in Italian gave the same picture as the English one with the same seed. Short prompts lose detail in any language.

How do I combine several images in one prompt?

Refer to them as image 1, image 2 and so on, in order, and say what each one gives: "Place the sneaker from image 2 on the rug in image 1, keep the room from image 1 unchanged".
