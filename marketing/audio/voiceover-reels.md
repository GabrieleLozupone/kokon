# Voiceover script: vertical reels (2026-10-21)

Four reels for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, 15–25 s each. Same voice and delivery as the 0.10
video (Chatterbox base, built-in voice, one sentence at a time). Every reel shows something only a local app can
show (offline, zero setup, the app itself); claims stay honest (12 GB GPU or Apple Silicon, non-commercial
images are said in the captions, not in the voice). "Prompt Plus" and "kokon dot app" are spelled for the voice.

Reels `offline` and `setup` are storyboards until Gabriele records the real screen; `founder` uses a placeholder
avatar until Gabriele films himself (his own voice then replaces this one).
